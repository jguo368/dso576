Read the data carefully

Language share = observed primary-language count ÷ complete, positive county total. The denominator requires all eight age/sex cells. Suppressed or absent values are unavailable, never zero; suppressed values are not reconstructed. Unknown and Missing/Unknown are separate categories.

P = preliminary (may be revised); F = final. Population comparisons support further review. They do not measure interpreter demand, provider capacity, or proven service shortages.
