Read the data carefully

Source: bundled DHCS “By Primary Language, Certified Eligibles” CSV. This view uses named county records only; statewide and invalid-county records are excluded. Counts describe certified eligibility, not provider availability or evidence of an access barrier.

Exact county language shares are unavailable: a verified county-total denominator is not included. Published language counts must not be summed into an exact county denominator. Unknown and Missing/Unknown remain separate categories. Status codes are shown as provided, without inferred definitions.
