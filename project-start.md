# Project Starting Point

## Selected project area

Medi-Cal Access and Provider-Capacity Planning

## Why our team is interested in it

Our team has a strong shared interest in healthcare and public policy from different backgrounds. One member studied Global Health as an undergraduate and is still on the pre-med track; another has long been interested in becoming a physician and previously worked as a data analytics intern at a pharmaceutical company; and another became interested in public health through undergraduate coursework. These experiences made us especially interested in exploring how data can improve healthcare access and provider capacity for Medi-Cal populations.

## Candidate Vision A

A Medi-Cal workforce planning manager at the California Department of Health Care Services must decide which counties and provider specialties should be prioritized for provider recruitment support.

## Candidate Vision B

A Medi-Cal access strategy manager at the California Department of Health Care Services must decide which counties and language populations should be prioritized for additional language-access capacity. The manager can select a primary language and eligibility month in an interface, then interact with a California county map to review the relevant certified-eligible counts and figures for each county.
